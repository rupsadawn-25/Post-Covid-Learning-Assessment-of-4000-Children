# Post-Covid-Learning-Assessment-of-4000-Children

Analysed the education compatibility of 4000 rural children from 4 different staes of India. CRY- Child Rights and You in collaboration with RILM ( Rotary India Literacy Mission) has implemented a project for child development in selected areas. Based on that we did a study to show that regardless of the age, the children are much underdeveloped in terms of education compatibility especially after the Covid breakout.
